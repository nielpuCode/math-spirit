Guide

How to Play

Answer math questions as fast as you can. Tap one of the 6 answers. Fast and correct wins.

▶ Playing Solo

  1. Number of Questions: how many questions one game has (1-500).
  2. Endless Mode: turn on to play without stopping.
  3. Hard Mode: turn on for trickier questions (harder kinds show up more often). The host's choice sets the whole room.
  4. Tap Play Solo, then tap an answer for each question.
  5. Tap Play Again for another round, or Home to go back.

👑 Hosting (Create Room)

  1. Type your nickname (🎲 gives you a random one).
  2. Set the number of questions and the hurry-up timer.
  3. Tap Create Room to get a 6-letter room code.
  4. Tap Copy Code or Copy Invite Link and send it to your friends. Show Room QR works too.

🚪 Joining a Friend's Room

  1. Type your nickname, type the room code, then tap Join Room.
  2. Inside the room you can still change your nickname: type it and tap Save.
  3. Tap I'm Ready ✓. The game starts when everyone is ready.
  4. After the game: Rematch plays again with the same room, Home leaves.

🔤 What Each Thing Means

  • Room code: 6 letters that identify one game room. Everyone in the room sees it.
  • Copy Invite Link / Show Room QR: inside a room, anyone can share these. Opening the link (or scanning the QR) joins the room directly, no typing. Use the live https link, since a localhost/dev address only works on your own computer.
  • Players: who is in the room right now (max 8). ✓ means that player is ready.
  • Hurry-up timer: there is no fixed time per question. Take as long as you want, until someone else answers. Then everyone left gets this many seconds (0 = off), or the question locks in blank.
  • Quit / Leave: stop the current game and exit.
  • Leaderboard: final ranking. Most correct wins, then accuracy, then total answering time.
  • Share via QR Code: shows a QR code so friends can open this page on their phones.
